Next steps

The first 30 days after launch

1
Delivery check
Over the first three days we confirm impressions, clicks, leads and calls are flowing, that web leads are landing in AgencyZoom, and that the commercial inbound line rings through.
2
Dispositions in AgencyZoom
Mark each lead as contacted, quoted or bound as it is worked. Before each review we send a short export guide; the export is imported into GOAL so the review reports on contact rate, quote rate and cost per policy.
3
Business mix
Industry type cannot be filtered, so at the first review we look at the business types coming through and adjust targeting where the data supports it.
4
Review cadence
Reviews run every two weeks. The first 30 days are judged on contact rate and quote rate; cost per policy is reported once policies have had time to bind.
