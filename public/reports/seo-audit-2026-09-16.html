01

Summary

Current state, the three changes that move lead volume, and what the redesign must preserve

The site reports 3 to 5 inbound leads per week. Google has 2 pages of kayrichinsure.com indexed, both targeting the phrase "better insurance rates." No page names a city or a line of business. The Google Business Profile lists the website as krichagency.com, a domain that does not resolve, so a Website click from the map fails. The profile has 5 reviews; the nearest State Farm competitor has 321. The social icons on the site link to facebook.com and instagram.com home pages rather than the agency's profiles.

Three changes carry most of the path to 6 to 15 leads per week. First, repair the local layer on the current site: Business Profile URL, one consistent name, address and phone everywhere, and a review request at every bind. Second, build the redesign as separate pages per line of business and per town, with the quote form in the first screen. Third, add a Texas race car insurance page on kayrichinsure.com so racing traffic is captured before it is handed to the Laris Motorsports partner.

The redesign keeps the domain and the two indexed URLs (301 redirects), keeps name, address and phone identical to the Business Profile, points every canonical at the production domain, and upgrades the existing LocalBusiness schema to InsuranceAgency.
