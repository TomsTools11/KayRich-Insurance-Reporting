Keyword research on September 19, 2026 produced 162 recommended keywords in 17 clusters, mapped to a 13-page sitemap. That is the 12 routes already built in the redesign demo plus one proposed Mabank page. Each page has a title tag, H1, and meta description. It starts at section 04. 150 of the 162 phrases came back from Google's autocomplete.

Three findings shape the copy. Google returned no suggestions for any Kaufman home, life, or business seed, so the measured local demand sits in car insurance and in Forney and Terrell. Texas questions are among the most repeated phrases ("how much is car insurance in texas per month" came back in 7 lookups), and 1 of 5 line pages in the demo has an FAQ block to answer them. The web search for "race car insurance texas" returned national carriers and no Texas agency.

The on-page review of the demo found 8 items (3 high, 3 medium, 2 low), listed in section 03. Speed, the Business Profile, citations, and measurement are in the Website SEO Audit of September 16.

03

Recommendations

On-page and copy items for the demo, ordered by priority · effort S = under 1 hour, M = a few hours, L = a day or more

Each item comes from comparing the demo's pages with what Google suggests. None changes the design or the route plan except item 6, which is marked for sign-off. Drafted title tags, H1s, and meta descriptions are in section 05.

1
HighEffort S
Settle the carrier wording before launch
Remove "Independent-minded" from the homepage meta description until the carrier relationship is confirmed. If the agency is a Farmers agency, add Farmers to the About page and the homepage intro under Farmers brand rules. The draft meta description in section 05 avoids both words.
Evidence: the owner email on the live site is a farmersagent.com address; the demo never says "Farmers"; its homepage meta description opens with "Independent-minded"; one of the two awards it cites is from Southern Farm Bureau Life. 9 keywords on the list depend on the answer.
2
HighEffort M
Add a Texas FAQ block to the Auto, Home insurance, Business, and Race car pages
Use the question keywords as the FAQ headings, word for word, and answer each in two to four sentences. Cost questions get rating factors and a Texas Department of Insurance citation, not an average. This also lifts each page toward the 400 to 700 words the Sept 16 audit set.
Evidence: question keywords mapped: Auto 16, Home insurance 16, Business 4, Race car 6. Only the Life page has an FAQ today, and its four questions already match phrases Google suggests. Line pages carry 213–287 words.
3
HighEffort S
Lead the Auto page with "car insurance"
Change the title tag to "Car Insurance in Kaufman, TX | KayRich Insurance Agency" and the H1 to "Car and truck insurance in Kaufman, TX." Keep the URL /auto-insurance/ and use "auto insurance" in an H2 and the intro.
Evidence: "car insurance kaufman tx" came back in 4 lookups; "auto insurance kaufman tx" in 1. "car insurance" also came back for Forney (4 lookups), Terrell (3), and Mabank (2). "auto insurance" came back once each for Forney and Terrell.
4
MediumEffort S
Shorten 6 meta descriptions and 3 title tags
Keep meta descriptions at 160 characters or fewer and titles at 60 or fewer so Google shows them whole. Drafts for every page are in section 05. Most of the saving comes from listing four towns instead of six.
Evidence: meta descriptions over 160: Home page 215, Race car 181, About 179, Life 170, Auto 165, Home insurance 165. Titles over 60: About 71, Race car 66, Service area 65.
5
MediumEffort M
Give the Forney and Terrell pages headings that name a line
Add "Car insurance in Forney" and "Home insurance in Forney" sections to the Forney page, and "Car insurance in Terrell" to the Terrell page. Two or three sentences each, linked to the line page. The scenario blocks already on the pages stay.
Evidence: Forney holds 13 keywords, 11 of them Strong, and is the only town where Google suggests home insurance. The demo Forney page is about 150 words and no heading names a line. "insurance companies in terrell texas" came back in 9 lookups, the most of any phrase.
6
MediumEffort M
Add a Mabank page, once the agency confirms it writes there
Build /service-area/mabank/ on the Forney and Terrell template. Kemp and Crandall stay as blocks on the service-area page. This adds a route to the approved plan, so it needs sign-off first.
Evidence: Google suggests "insurance mabank tx", "car insurance mabank tx", and "car insurance gun barrel city tx". Kemp returned only named agencies. Crandall returned one Allstate phrase.
7
LowEffort S
Use the full question wording in the Life FAQ
Change "How much do I need?" to "How much life insurance do I need?" and "Do I need a medical exam?" to a heading that includes "life insurance". The answers stay as written.
Evidence: Google suggests "how much life insurance do i need", "life insurance no medical exam", "is life insurance through work enough", and "life insurance for children". The demo headings drop the words "life insurance".
8
LowEffort S
Match the award and the owner name across the live site and the demo
Pick the name the owner publishes under and the awards that can be attributed, then use them on the About and Life pages and in schema.
Evidence: live site: "Named Top Agent for Life Insurance in 2025" and "Kayleigh Richards". Demo: "2023 President's Council" and "Kayleigh Phillips-Richards". The Life and About meta descriptions both cite the 2023 recognition.

Scope & method

What this report covers

Pages reviewedThe 12 page files in the redesign demo (kayrich-site-demo.vercel.app), read from the demo build on September 19, 2026: title tag, meta description, headings, word count, and FAQ blocks. The live homepage and /book-online were fetched the same day to confirm their title tags and H1s are unchanged since the September 16 audit.
Keyword research224 seed phrases covering the lines on the live site and the demo, the six towns the demo names plus nearby towns, Farmers brand terms, brand names, and Texas topics (hail, wind, roof, foundation, flood, tornado, state minimums, uninsured motorist, PIP, SR-22, workers comp). Each seed was run through Google's public autocomplete (US, English) on September 19, 2026. Five head terms were also searched to see who ranks today.
Demand signalStrong = Google suggested the phrase in 2 or more separate lookups. Present = 1 lookup. Not in autocomplete = no suggestion came back, and the phrase stays only where the page needs it. The keyword list names the seed lookups that returned each phrase, and the workbook's Autocomplete log tab holds every raw result.
No search volumeNo keyword-volume tool (Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush) was connected. The demand signal shows that people search a phrase. It does not rank phrases by volume. Run the primary keywords through Keyword Planner before final sign-off if volumes are needed.
Not includedGoogle Search Console and Google Analytics data (no access), so current rankings, impressions, and search traffic are unknown. No traffic or lead estimate is attached to any keyword.
How this fits the Sept 16 auditThe audit recommended 36 terms from a first autocomplete pass and set the route plan. This research reran the pass with 224 seeds, kept the routes, and extends the list to 162 keywords with a role and a placement for each. Where the two differ, this report is the newer one.
